Questions & known gaps

The same tracked standards, organised as a service designer actually needs them: by the question you're trying to ask, grouped by domain, with source, currency, and licence shown for each. Only standards that publish an actual question or item (including multi-item screening instruments like PHQ-9) appear here — administrative identifiers and coded flags (e.g. NHS Number) still live on the standards view. Entries with no usable standard are listed as known gaps rather than silently omitted.
